Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy describes what Railwatch Cloud at railwatch.rebulk.com collects, how it is stored, and what we do with it. The Service is operated by Rebulk.

What we collect

Account information. Your name, email address, and a password hash. If you sign in with Google, we store the Google account identifier and the email address it verifies.

Telemetry. Whatever the Railwatch gem in your application sends us: requests, background jobs, database queries, exceptions, logs, cache activity, outgoing HTTP calls, deploys, and host metrics. This can include request parameters, error messages, SQL, user identifiers, and log lines from your application. You control what is sent.

Operational data. Server logs with IP addresses and request metadata, kept for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics on the Service.

Redaction before data leaves your application

The gem redacts on your servers, before anything is transmitted to us:

  • Authorization, cookie, and other sensitive headers are removed.
  • Request parameters are filtered using your Rails filter_parameters configuration, so anything hidden from your logs is hidden from us too.
  • You can extend redaction with redact_params, redact_headers, and per-record redact_* hooks described in the gem documentation.

Redaction is your responsibility. We store what we receive.

How data is stored

  • One database per environment. Each application environment's telemetry lives in its own SQLite database file. Telemetry from one account is never written to or read from another account's database, and an environment token can only select the single database it was issued for.
  • Encrypted in transit over TLS.
  • Hosted on our servers in the United States on Hetzner infrastructure, with continuous off-site replication to object storage for recovery.
  • Bounded. Oversized values are truncated at ingest and every stored string is limited in size.

How long we keep it

Telemetry is pruned after the retention period on your plan (seven days on the free plan). When you delete an environment or an account, its telemetry databases are removed immediately. Off-site backups age out within seven days. Account records are deleted with the account.

How we use it

We use your data only to provide the Service to you: to show you your own telemetry, to send alerts and digests you configure, and to keep the Service running. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to train models. We may look at aggregate, non-identifying usage to understand load.

We disclose data only if required by law or to protect the Service and its users.

Third parties

We use Hetzner for hosting, an object-storage provider for backups, and Amazon SES to send email. If you connect an integration such as Slack or Linear, we send the data you configure to that service under its own terms.

Your choices

You can export or delete your data by deleting environments or your account from settings. You can stop sending data at any time by removing the gem or pausing an environment. For anything else, email cole@rebulk.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted on this page. The date at the top reflects the last revision.
